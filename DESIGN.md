# Design direction: Imperal Network × Slice of Life

## Design Read
Reading this as: a community landing page for Minecraft players, in a grounded dark-fantasy language with forest-stone architecture and a restrained amethyst portal motif, dial ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2.

## Identity
The site introduces a fantasy network and its Slice of Life collaboration. The page should feel like a quiet threshold into a world that is still being built, not a store claiming to be live.

## Visual language
- Background: near-black green, like a shaded forest at night.
- Main text: warm stone-white for comfortable reading.
- Accent: antique gold for important actions and status; pale amethyst for the portal and fantasy headings.
- Typography: system sans-serif for readable interface text, Georgia for editorial fantasy headings. No external font download.
- Motif: an arched gate with layered landscape shapes. The motif connects the hero and section symbols without using a fake official logo or stock illustration.
- Composition: asymmetrical hero, varied panels for Fantasy and Slice of Life, then quieter information sections. Whitespace separates story beats.

## Motion
- MOTION 2: a one-time entrance transition on the hero only, plus short hover/focus transitions.
- No perpetual floating, pulsing, parallax, or background video.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

## Performance and accessibility
- Plain HTML, CSS, and small vanilla JavaScript. No framework or third-party runtime.
- No external images, icon packs, or web fonts.
- Mobile-first layout, visible keyboard focus, native details/summary FAQ, semantic landmarks, and links to real destinations only.
- No server address, Discord invite, price, rank benefit, online count, or payment feature is invented. Missing details are labelled clearly.
